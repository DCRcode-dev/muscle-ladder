DCR Gym Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

DCR Gym is a personal workout tracker. It is built for and used by its owner only, and it is not offered to the public.

What the app stores

Workouts you log (exercises, sets, weights, reps, dates and durations), notes, body measurements and app settings. This data is kept on your own device and backed up to a Google Sheet in the owner's own Google account.

Google Health data

If you connect Google Health, the app reads sleep and health measurement data (such as resting heart rate, heart rate variability and oxygen saturation) with read-only access, to show recovery and readiness inside the app. This data is stored only in the owner's own Google Sheet and Apps Script. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone. There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

Google API data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account's security settings. Deleting the Google Sheet and clearing the app's storage on your device removes the data.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for DCR Gym.
